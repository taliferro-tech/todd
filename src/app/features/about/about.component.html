A Momentum System, not a CRM

Given what you know right now, what should you do next?

A CRM stores history. 16Ahead watches what is happening across your work, surfaces the signal that matters, and helps you choose the next action before momentum disappears. When your work moves forward, you become a more capable version of yourself.

What it connects

The parts of work that are easy to separate

Contacts, emails, documents, feedback, tasks and signals usually live in different places. 16Ahead connects them so you can see what matters, notice where work is slowing down, and act before an opportunity becomes a loss.
